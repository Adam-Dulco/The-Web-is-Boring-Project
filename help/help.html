Requesting items to view

To request archive items, please make a note of the full reference code shown in the catalogue entry.

You should then contact the archive service in advance so that material can be prepared for your visit.

Some items may require additional notice or may only be produced under supervised conditions.

Levels of Description

Our archive catalogues use different levels of description to structure a collection and place records in context. This helps show where records came from, how they relate to one another, and how they were originally organised.

This forms a hierarchy, where most levels sit beneath a parent level above them.

Collection
A collection consists of records created, received and used by a particular organisation, family or individual.
Section
A subdivision of a collection. Sections may reflect broad areas of an institution, such as governance or alumni, or a specific department.
Series
Records that share content or format, or that were originally kept together and/or have a shared origin.
Sub-series
Records forming a clearly defined series within a broader series.
Item
A single unit, such as a file, a volume, a photograph or an individual piece of paper.
Piece
In some instances, an item may be divided for descriptive purposes into separately numbered pieces.
